What you get

Every game, automatically

GG Stats notices when a Battle.net or ShieldBattery game ends and analyzes it on its own. No buttons to press.

The whole game

Scoreboard, army and economy over time, supply blocks, bases, unit breakdowns, and every player's build order with timings.

Your own stats

Your record by matchup and map, your macro, who you play with and against, and how it all moves over your last hundred games.

Old replays too

Point it at your replay folders and it builds a library you can browse, filter and analyze.

How it works

  1. You finish a game. StarCraft saves the replay like it always does.
  2. GG Stats plays it back. A replay only holds each player's commands, so GG Stats starts a second, invisible StarCraft from your install and fast forwards through the game, reading its numbers as it goes. It runs at low priority, so it won't lag your next game.
  3. The stats are ready. Usually 10 to 30 seconds later. You get a notification if the app is in the tray.

Questions

Does it touch the game I'm playing?

No. It only ever loads replays, in a separate StarCraft it starts itself. It never injects into a game you play, and it never joins online games.

Do I have to close StarCraft?

No. The analysis runs next to it, and you don't need to be logged in to Battle.net.

Windows says the installer isn't safe.

The installer isn't signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. The code is all on GitHub if you want to check it or build it yourself.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Replays and stats stay on your computer. The only thing it asks the internet is whether there's a new version, and you can turn that off.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. It needs Windows 10 or 11.
